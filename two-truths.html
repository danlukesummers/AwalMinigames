Two Truths & One Lie

Two true. One lie. Ask the right questions, find the fib.

Open a new case

Everyone shares two truths and one lie about themselves. Others ask follow-up questions out loud, then click the lie.

You vs one student (or two students). Each tells once and the other clicks the lie. You only need one student to play.

If you play, you take a turn in the hot seat and guess along with the class, so one student is enough to start.

Spoken: players tap the mic, say each statement, and the browser types it for them. They can fix mistakes before submitting. Works best in Chrome, Edge and Safari.
