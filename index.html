FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do game credits actually work? +
Each time you launch a live game session, it uses 1 credit — no matter how many students join or how long it runs. Free plan users get 5 credits every week — up to 20 per month — refreshed automatically. Unlimited plan users never think about credits again.
Do my students need to create an account? +
No. Students click your invite link or enter your room code in their browser — no sign-up, no password, no download. Under 10 seconds to join.
What devices and browsers does AWAL work on? +
AWAL runs entirely in the browser — laptop, Chromebook, tablet, or smartphone, no installs. Chrome, Edge, or Safari for the smoothest experience.
How does the AI vocabulary generation work? +
Type a topic and pick a CEFR level from A1 to C2 — AWAL instantly generates a matched word bank, quiz, or prompt list. Edit before you start, or save it for later.
Can I use it for 1-on-1 and group classes? +
Yes — every game supports 1 up to 6 players in the same room, so it works equally well for private tutoring and small group or academy classes.

Our story

We built the classroom tool we wished we had.

AWAL started with one frustrated online tutor, a muted student, and a slide deck nobody was looking at. Three years later, it's how thousands of ESL teachers get their students talking.

The best lesson I ever taught had no slides at all — just a game, a timer, and six kids talking over each other to win.

— AWAL founding team, on the moment this started

How it started

It was 9pm on a Tuesday, halfway through a 1-on-1 online English lesson, when our founder realized they'd been talking for eleven minutes straight. The student — polite, capable, clearly bored — had said maybe forty words the entire class. The slide deck was beautiful. It was also completely one-directional.

That's the quiet failure mode of online ESL teaching: the tools we inherited from in-person classrooms and corporate presentations were never built for conversation. They're built for delivery. A teacher reading slides at a student isn't language practice — it's a lecture with extra steps.

So the first version of AWAL wasn't a company. It was a scrappy Guess Who clone, built over a weekend, just to get one shy 14-year-old student to describe a face out loud instead of clicking "next." It worked. Not because the game was clever — because winning required speaking, and nothing else did.

Word got around a teacher Discord server. Then a Facebook group for freelance tutors. Within a few months, teachers from academies across three continents were asking for word searches, trivia, whiteboards — anything that turned their screen into a room students actually wanted to talk in.

Today, AWAL is built by online teachers, for online teachers — every feature shipped because a real tutor asked for it during an 11pm lesson-prep panic. We're still small. We still read every piece of feedback. And we still believe the best measure of a good ESL lesson isn't how good the slides looked — it's how much the student talked.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

1. What we collect

When you create a teacher account, we collect your name, email address, and billing information (for paid plans, processed by our payment provider — we never store full card numbers). We also collect usage data such as game sessions launched and features used.

Students joining a game room provide only a display name for that session — no account, email, or password is required or stored beyond the session.

2. How we use it

  • To operate and improve AWAL's core features
  • To process AI-generated vocabulary and quiz requests
  • To send account, billing, and product update emails
  • To understand aggregate usage patterns and fix bugs

3. Student data

AWAL is designed to minimize data collected from student participants. Display names entered during a game session are not linked to any persistent profile and are cleared shortly after the session ends.

4. Sharing & processors

We share data only with service providers necessary to run AWAL — such as payment processing and cloud hosting — under contracts that require them to protect your data. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

5. Data retention

Teacher account data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request account deletion at any time, after which your personal data is removed within a reasonable period, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encryption in transit — to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, and we encourage strong, unique passwords on your account.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@chatterbox.example.
