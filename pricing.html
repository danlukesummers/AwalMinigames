Billing questions

The short answers. Full FAQ is on the home page.

Can I switch between monthly and annual? +
Yes — upgrade or switch billing cycles anytime from your account settings. Any remaining time on your current plan is credited automatically.
What happens if I cancel? +
You'll keep Unlimited access until the end of your current billing period, then drop back to the Free plan with 5 credits/week (20/month max) — no data lost.
Do you offer academy or team discounts? +
Academies managing multiple teachers can reach out for volume pricing — Academy Mode is on our roadmap with dedicated plans coming soon.
