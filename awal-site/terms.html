Terms of Service

Last updated: August 14, 2026

1. Accounts

To use AWAL as a teacher, you need an account with a valid email address. You're responsible for keeping your login credentials secure and for all activity that happens under your account.

You must be at least 18 years old, or the age of majority in your country, to create a teacher account. Student participants join game rooms without creating an account.

2. Plans & credits

AWAL offers a Free plan (5 game credits per week, up to 20 per month) and paid Unlimited plans (Monthly at €3/month, Annual at €24/year). One credit is consumed each time a game session is launched, regardless of session length or number of participants.

  • Free plan credits reset weekly (5 per week), capped at 20 total per month, and do not roll over.
  • Unlimited plan subscribers are not subject to credit limits for as long as their subscription is active.
  • Prices are shown in EUR and may be subject to local tax.

3. Content you create

Custom vocabulary lists, word banks, and whiteboard drawings you create remain yours. By using AI-generation features, you grant AWAL permission to process your topic and level inputs in order to generate content on your behalf.

4. Students in your room

As the teacher, you are responsible for obtaining any necessary consent for minors participating in your game rooms, in accordance with your local regulations and your school or academy's policies.

5. Acceptable use

AWAL may not be used to create or share content that is illegal, harmful, or violates the rights of others. We reserve the right to suspend accounts that violate this policy.

6. Cancellation & refunds

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period; no partial refunds are issued for unused time, except where required by law.

7. Liability

AWAL is provided "as is." We work to keep the platform reliable, but we're not liable for indirect or incidental damages arising from use of the service, to the extent permitted by law.

8. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by email or in-app notice before they take effect.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

1. What we collect

When you create a teacher account, we collect your name, email address, and billing information (for paid plans, processed by our payment provider — we never store full card numbers). We also collect usage data such as game sessions launched and features used.

Students joining a game room provide only a display name for that session — no account, email, or password is required or stored beyond the session.

2. How we use it

  • To operate and improve AWAL's core features
  • To process AI-generated vocabulary and quiz requests
  • To send account, billing, and product update emails
  • To understand aggregate usage patterns and fix bugs

3. Student data

AWAL is designed to minimize data collected from student participants. Display names entered during a game session are not linked to any persistent profile and are cleared shortly after the session ends.

4. Sharing & processors

We share data only with service providers necessary to run AWAL — such as payment processing and cloud hosting — under contracts that require them to protect your data. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

5. Data retention

Teacher account data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request account deletion at any time, after which your personal data is removed within a reasonable period, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encryption in transit — to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, and we encourage strong, unique passwords on your account.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@chatterbox.example.
