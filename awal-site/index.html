FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do game credits actually work? +
Each time you launch a live game session, it uses 1 credit — no matter how many students join or how long it runs. Free plan users get 5 credits every week — up to 20 per month — refreshed automatically. Unlimited plan users never think about credits again.
Do my students need to create an account? +
No. Students click your invite link or enter your room code in their browser — no sign-up, no password, no download. Under 10 seconds to join.
What devices and browsers does AWAL work on? +
AWAL runs entirely in the browser — laptop, Chromebook, tablet, or smartphone, no installs. Chrome, Edge, or Safari for the smoothest experience.
How does the AI vocabulary generation work? +
Type a topic and pick a CEFR level from A1 to C2 — AWAL instantly generates a matched word bank, quiz, or prompt list. Edit before you start, or save it for later.
Can I use it for 1-on-1 and group classes? +
Yes — every game supports 1 up to 6 players in the same room, so it works equally well for private tutoring and small group or academy classes.
